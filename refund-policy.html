01

Introduction

MATCH-BOX operates a 4-a-side football facility and provides football-related services that may include football pitch bookings, football coaching, football tournaments, private football games, birthday football events, school and student football events, corporate football events, and group football experiences.

This Refund Policy explains how cancellation and refund requests are reviewed and handled for bookings, registrations, and event enquiries made through the MATCH-BOX website and official communication channels.

This policy should be read together with the MATCH-BOX Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Disclaimer. Where a specific service has its own published conditions, those conditions apply in addition to this policy.

02

Scope of This Policy

This Refund Policy applies to:

  • Football pitch booking requests and confirmed bookings.
  • Football coaching registrations.
  • Football tournament team registrations.
  • Event enquiries and confirmed private bookings.
  • Any fees or charges collected by MATCH-BOX in connection with these services.

This policy does not cover third-party services that may be independently operated by other organisations. Where applicable, those services are governed by their own terms and refund policies.

03

Payment Method & Refund Basis

The initial payment method for MATCH-BOX pitch bookings is Pay at Venue. Online payments are not part of the initial implementation. Where a booking or registration is completed and paid at the venue, any refund will be handled based on the confirmed payment made and the applicable conditions in this policy.

Where any additional service introduces a different payment method (for example, online payment in a future phase), the applicable refund basis for that service will be published alongside that service.

The initial pricing for pitch bookings is ₹350/hour for non-peak slots and ₹500/hour for peak slots. Peak periods are configured by the administration and shown during booking.
04

Pitch Booking Refunds

Football pitch bookings are one-hour sessions. Cancellation and refund requests for pitch bookings are handled according to the conditions below, subject to the approved cancellation policy.

4.1 Customer-Requested Cancellation

  • Customers should contact MATCH-BOX as early as possible if a booking needs to be cancelled.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the time between the cancellation request and the booked slot.
  • Where a booking was not yet paid, cancellation may result in the booking request being closed.

4.2 Rescheduling

  • Rescheduling requests are subject to availability.
  • Approved rescheduling may avoid a cancellation where an alternative slot is available.
  • Any rescheduling is confirmed only when MATCH-BOX confirms the new slot.

05

Coaching Registration Refunds

Football coaching registrations are subject to session capacity, review, and confirmation. The current coaching session information is:

  • Morning session: 5:00 AM – 6:30 AM, 90 minutes, 10-player capacity (8 outfield + 2 goalkeepers).
  • Afternoon session: 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM, 90 minutes, capacity configurable by the administrator.

5.1 Cancellation by the Player or Guardian

  • Cancellation requests should be submitted through the official MATCH-BOX contact channel.
  • Refund eligibility for coaching registrations depends on the approved coaching refund conditions.
  • Where a coaching fee has not been paid, cancellation may result in the registration being closed.

5.2 Cancellation by MATCH-BOX

  • If MATCH-BOX cancels a coaching session, affected players will be informed through the contact details provided during registration.
  • Where a coaching fee has been paid, the applicable refund or alternative arrangement will be communicated by MATCH-BOX.

5.3 Coaching Refund Conditions

Development placeholder. Specific coaching refund conditions, deadlines, percentages, and any non-refundable amounts must be approved by the business owner before publication. Coaching fees are not currently published on the website.
06

Tournament Refunds

Tournament participation is subject to each tournament's published requirements, registration deadlines, and capacity. The following applies to tournament registrations:

6.1 Team Withdrawal

  • A team withdrawing from a tournament should notify MATCH-BOX as early as possible.
  • Refund eligibility for tournament fees depends on the tournament's published withdrawal conditions.
  • Where a tournament has not yet closed registration, an alternative arrangement may be discussed with MATCH-BOX.

6.2 Tournament Cancellation by MATCH-BOX

  • If MATCH-BOX cancels a tournament, affected teams will be informed through the captain's contact details.
  • Where a tournament entry fee has been paid, the applicable refund or alternative arrangement will be communicated by MATCH-BOX.

6.3 Tournament Refund Conditions

Development placeholder. Tournament entry fees, withdrawal deadlines, refund percentages, and any non-refundable amounts must be published per tournament by the business owner. Tournament fees are not currently published on the website.

Event Enquiry Refunds

Event enquiries for birthday football parties, private football games, school and student events, corporate football events, and group football experiences are subject to review and approval. Submitting an enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.

7.1 Event Cancellation

  • If a confirmed event is cancelled by the customer, refund eligibility depends on the approved event cancellation conditions.
  • If MATCH-BOX cancels a confirmed event, affected customers will be informed through their provided contact details.
  • Where a payment has been made for a confirmed event, the applicable refund or alternative arrangement will be communicated by MATCH-BOX.

7.2 Event Refund Conditions

Development placeholder. Event-specific cancellation deadlines, refund percentages, and any non-refundable charges must be communicated during the event confirmation process. Event fees are not currently published on the website.

Business-Initiated Cancellations

MATCH-BOX may need to cancel or reschedule a booking, coaching session, tournament, or confirmed event due to unforeseen circumstances, operational requirements, or facility issues.

  • Affected customers will be informed through the contact details provided during booking or registration.
  • Where possible, an alternative slot or arrangement will be offered.
  • Where a payment has been made, the applicable refund or alternative arrangement will be discussed with the affected customer.
Development placeholder. The specific refund, rescheduling, or compensation process for business-initiated cancellations must be approved by the business owner before publication.
09

No-Shows & Late Arrivals

Customers are expected to arrive on time for their confirmed booking or registration. The following general conditions apply:

  • A no-show for a confirmed booking may result in the booking being treated as used, subject to the approved policy.
  • Late arrival may reduce the available playing time where the slot cannot be extended.
  • Late arrival does not automatically create a right to a refund or rescheduling.
Development placeholder. Specific no-show and late-arrival conditions, including any partial refund or credit arrangements, must be approved by the business owner before publication.
10

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation, please follow these steps:

  • Contact MATCH-BOX through the official contact page.
  • Include your booking reference, registration reference, or event enquiry reference where available.
  • Provide the name, contact details, and the reason for the cancellation or refund request.
  • Include any relevant supporting information that may help MATCH-BOX review the request.
Requests are reviewed by MATCH-BOX based on the applicable conditions. Submission of a request does not automatically guarantee approval.
11

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, MATCH-BOX will communicate the refund method and any relevant information to the customer.

  • Refunds are processed only after a request has been reviewed and approved by MATCH-BOX.
  • The refund method may depend on the original payment method and applicable business arrangements.
  • Processing time depends on the payment method and the applicable conditions.
Development placeholder. Specific refund processing times, methods, and any related conditions must be approved by the business owner before publication. Refund processing times have not been defined in this draft.
12

Exceptions & Special Cases

MATCH-BOX may review cancellation and refund requests on a case-by-case basis where:

  • A cancellation is caused by an unforeseen circumstance beyond the customer's reasonable control.
  • A booking or registration error is identified and can be reasonably resolved.
  • A business-initiated change affects a confirmed booking, registration, or event.
  • Any other situation applies where a fair and reasonable resolution is appropriate.

Any decision on an exception is made by MATCH-BOX and communicated to the customer through the official communication channels.
