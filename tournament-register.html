    REGISTRATION RULES

    IMPORTANT REGISTRATION INFORMATION.

    01

    Select an Open Tournament

    Only tournaments with open registration can be selected. Closed, full, or completed tournaments cannot be registered for.

    02

    Submit Accurate Information

    Provide accurate team, captain, and player information. The captain is the primary contact for all tournament communication.

    03

    Follow Squad Rules

    Add players according to the selected tournament's squad rules. Squad size limits are confirmed by MATCH-BOX.

    04

    Review Before Submitting

    Review all details before submission. Use the review step to correct any mistakes.

    05

    Wait for Confirmation

    Submitting a registration does not automatically confirm your team. MATCH-BOX reviews each registration and provides confirmation.

    06

    Tournament-Specific Requirements

    Each tournament may have its own registration requirements. Please review the tournament details before submitting your team.

    TOURNAMENT REGISTRATION FAQ

    Select an open tournament on this page, complete the team and captain details, build your squad, review the information, and submit the registration.

    Each registration is submitted for one tournament at a time. If you wish to register for another tournament, you can submit a separate registration.

    Squad size limits depend on the selected tournament's rules. MATCH-BOX uses a 4-a-side format, but the number of players on the pitch does not automatically define the squad size. Please review the tournament details for squad requirements.

    Substitute rules depend on the tournament's configuration. Please review the tournament details or contact MATCH-BOX for confirmation.

    Yes. Use the "Captain is also a team player" checkbox in the squad step to add the captain as a player without duplicating their details.

    Editing after submission depends on MATCH-BOX's review process. Please contact MATCH-BOX directly if you need to make changes.

    After submitting your registration, MATCH-BOX will review your details and contact the captain with the registration status.

    Once a tournament reaches its maximum team capacity, registration is closed. Contact MATCH-BOX to ask about upcoming tournaments.

    Please contact MATCH-BOX as soon as possible with your registration reference so the team can assist you.
