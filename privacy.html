01

Introduction

MATCH-BOX operates a 4-a-side football facility and provides football-related services. Depending on availability and published information, these services may include football pitch bookings, football coaching, football tournaments, private football games, birthday football events, school and student football events, corporate football events, and group football experiences.

This policy describes how personal information is handled when users interact with the MATCH-BOX website and services. It explains what information may be collected, why it is collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

02

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • The MATCH-BOX website.
  • Booking forms.
  • Coaching registration forms.
  • Tournament registration forms.
  • Event enquiry forms.
  • Contact forms.
  • Official communication channels used by the business, where applicable.

Separate third-party services may have their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover third-party platforms independently operated by other organisations.

03

Personal Information We Collect

The exact information collected depends on which service the user chooses. The categories below summarise the types of information that may be submitted.

A. Basic Contact Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number.

B. Booking Information

  • Booking date.
  • Selected time slot.
  • Booking reference.
  • Booking remarks.
  • Booking status.
  • Applicable booking details.

C. Coaching Information

  • Player name.
  • Date of birth or age, where collected.
  • Playing position.
  • Selected coaching session.
  • Player contact details.
  • Parent or guardian information where required.
  • Previous football experience, if voluntarily provided.

D. Tournament Information

  • Team name.
  • Team details.
  • Captain name.
  • Captain email and mobile number.
  • Player names and playing positions.
  • Squad information.
  • Tournament registration details.

E. Event Information

  • Event type.
  • Preferred date and time.
  • Organisation name, where relevant.
  • Expected participant count.
  • Event description.
  • Contact details.

F. Communication Information

  • Subject of enquiry.
  • Message content.
  • Communication preferences, if collected.
  • Enquiry reference and status.
04

Information Collected Through Bookings

When a customer books the football pitch, the following information may be collected:

  • Customer name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number.
  • Booking date.
  • Selected time slot.
  • Optional remarks.
  • Booking reference and status.

This information is used to:

  • Process booking requests.
  • Check availability.
  • Prevent double bookings.
  • Manage confirmed bookings.
  • Communicate booking updates.
  • Handle relevant customer enquiries.
The initial planned payment method is Pay at Venue. Online payment is not part of the initial implementation, and payment card information is not collected through the initial booking process.

  • Playing position.
  • Selected session.

This information may be used to:

  • Process coaching registrations.
  • Identify the selected session.
  • Manage session capacity.
  • Contact players or parents/guardians.
