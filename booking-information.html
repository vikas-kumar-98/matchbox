PITCH TIMINGS

CHOOSE THE RIGHT TIME FOR YOUR GAME.

The pitch operates every day from 6:00 AM to 12:00 Midnight, with one-hour booking slots.

6:00 AM 12:00 Midnight

Each block represents a one-hour slot. The final slot must finish by 12:00 Midnight.

  • Customers can select from the available one-hour slots.
  • The final slot must finish by midnight.
  • Past time slots cannot be booked.
  • Availability can change as bookings are confirmed.
  • Certain slots may be unavailable due to existing bookings or administrative blocks.
PITCH PRICING

SIMPLE & TRANSPARENT PITCH PRICING.

Two clear rates depending on the applicable booking period. The final price is confirmed during booking.

NON-PEAK HOURS
₹350 / Hour

Book your football session during applicable non-peak periods. The applicable rate is confirmed when you select your slot.

PEAK HOURS
₹500 / Hour

Peak-period football sessions are charged at the applicable peak hourly rate. Peak periods are configured by management.

Peak and non-peak periods are configured by MATCH-BOX management. The applicable price for your selected date and time will be displayed during the booking process. The backend calculates the final applicable amount.

PAYMENT

HOW DOES PAYMENT WORK?

The initial booking process uses a straightforward payment arrangement.

Current Payment Method: Pay at Venue

At this stage, MATCH-BOX uses a Pay at Venue payment arrangement. Customers can review their booking details and follow the confirmation instructions provided by MATCH-BOX.

Online payment functionality is not part of the initial booking process. Customers are not required to make an online payment to submit a booking request.

BOOKING CONFIRMATION

WHEN IS YOUR BOOKING CONFIRMED?

Understanding the difference between submitting a request and receiving confirmation.

STEP 1

Submit Your Details

The customer submits their selected date, time slot, and contact information through the booking page.

STEP 2

Booking Verification

The system checks slot availability and booking conditions before accepting the request.

STEP 3

Booking Confirmation

The customer receives the actual confirmation response once the booking has been successfully accepted.

Please rely on the confirmation provided by MATCH-BOX rather than assuming that an enquiry or incomplete submission has reserved your pitch. A booking request is not a confirmed booking.
