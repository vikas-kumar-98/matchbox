MATCH-BOX GROUND
TRAIN ON THE GROUND

A FOOTBALL GROUND BUILT FOR DEVELOPMENT.

MATCH-BOX coaching takes place on our dedicated 4-a-side football ground. Every session is designed to help players develop their game in a focused, football-first environment.

  • Small-sided football ground for focused practice
  • Structured sessions on a dedicated pitch
  • Opportunities to develop skills, teamwork, and game awareness
  • Coaching for outfield players and goalkeepers
REGISTER FOR COACHING
COACHING SESSIONS

CHOOSE YOUR TRAINING SESSION.

Select the session that suits your schedule. Both sessions run for 90 minutes and focus on developing football skills, teamwork, and game awareness.

MORNING

MORNING COACHING

  • Time: 5:00 AM – 6:30 AM
  • Duration: 90 minutes
  • Total capacity: 10 players
  • 8 outfield players + 2 goalkeepers

Morning sessions are structured around technical drills and small-sided practice on our football ground.

AFTERNOON

AFTERNOON COACHING

  • Time: 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM
  • Duration: 90 minutes
  • Capacity: Managed by MATCH-BOX

Afternoon sessions focus on structured practice and team-based football activities on the ground.
