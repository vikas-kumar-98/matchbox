General Facility Rules

These are the basic expectations for everyone using the MATCH-BOX football facility:

  • Follow the instructions provided by authorised MATCH-BOX staff.
  • Respect other players, visitors and facility personnel.
  • Use the football facility only for its intended football-related activities.
  • Follow the applicable booking, coaching, tournament or event conditions.
  • Do not interfere with another group's confirmed booking.
  • Keep the playing environment respectful and organised.
  • Report any safety concern or damage to facility staff.
02

Booking and Pitch Access Rules

Confirmed booking details:

  • Operating hours: 6:00 AM to 12:00 midnight.
  • Each booking slot: one hour.
  • Non-peak price: ₹350 per hour.
  • Peak price: ₹500 per hour.
  • Peak periods are configured by the administrator.
  • Customers should book through the official booking process.
  • Visitors should check their booking date and time carefully.
  • Customers should use only their confirmed booking period.
  • Customers should respect the end of their booked slot.
  • Customers should not occupy the pitch during another group's booking.
  • Requests to extend a booking depend on actual availability and approval.
  • Booking changes and cancellations are subject to the applicable policy.
  • Only confirmed bookings should be treated as reserved pitch time.
Early-entry permissions, grace periods, overtime charges and extra booking fees have not been assumed on this page. These require business approval before being presented as binding rules.

03

Player Conduct and Fair Play

Football is at its best when it is played with respect. All players are expected to:

Respect Other Players
  • Treat teammates and opponents respectfully.
  • Avoid abusive language and personal insults.
  • Respect different experience and skill levels.
Play Fair
  • Compete honestly.
  • Respect fair-play principles.
  • Avoid intentional dangerous challenges.
  • Do not deliberately disrupt another game.
Maintain Team Spirit
  • Encourage teammates.
  • Avoid unnecessary arguments.
  • Accept disagreements respectfully.
  • Keep the game enjoyable for everyone.
Respect Staff
  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorised staff.
  • Raise concerns politely.
  • Do not threaten, intimidate or harass anyone.
04

Football Safety Guidelines

Football involves physical activity, and players should participate responsibly. General guidance includes:

  • Players should assess whether they are fit to participate.
  • Players should warm up appropriately before playing.
  • Avoid reckless tackles and dangerous physical contact.
  • Stop playing if an injury or safety concern occurs.
  • Inform MATCH-BOX staff about hazards or unsafe conditions.
  • Do not continue playing when doing so could put yourself or others at risk.
  • Follow any safety instructions provided by facility staff.
Safety message: Great football is competitive, but it should never come at the expense of another player's safety.
Business confirmation pending. MATCH-BOX does not claim to provide medical services, medical staff, first-aid equipment, insurance or emergency facilities unless these have been confirmed by the business owner.
05

Appropriate Football Equipment

Players should use suitable clothing and football equipment appropriate for the playing environment:

  • Wear comfortable sports clothing.
  • Use suitable football footwear for the actual playing surface.
  • Ensure footwear is in suitable condition.
  • Avoid accessories that could create unnecessary injury risks.
  • Follow any footwear or equipment instructions officially communicated by MATCH-BOX.
Business confirmation pending. A particular turf type, footwear model, boot type or mandatory equipment is not specified here. Changing rooms, lockers, showers and equipment-rental services have not been assumed.
06

Pitch and Equipment Care

Protecting the football facility helps keep the playing environment safe and ready for everyone:

  • Use the pitch and any provided equipment responsibly.
  • Do not intentionally damage goals, nets, markings or other facility property.
  • Do not misuse football equipment.
  • Do not move or alter facility equipment without permission.
  • Report visible damage or safety concerns to staff.
  • Avoid actions that may damage the playing surface.
Respect the pitch. Protect the game. Any equipment or amenities not confirmed by MATCH-BOX have not been assumed on this page.
07

Cleanliness and Personal Belongings

  • Dispose of waste responsibly using the designated arrangements available at the facility.
  • Do not intentionally litter the playing area.
  • Keep personal belongings organised.
  • Do not leave belongings in areas where they may obstruct activity.
  • Take personal belongings when leaving.
  • Inform staff if you find an unattended item.
Business confirmation pending. Lockers, storage facilities, changing areas and specific waste-management arrangements have not been assumed. For lost property, please contact MATCH-BOX through the official contact page.
08

Respect for Staff and Other Visitors

Every player and visitor is expected to contribute to a welcoming football environment:

  • Respect MATCH-BOX staff and their reasonable instructions.
  • Respect other players' booked time.
  • Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or intimidation.
  • Do not intentionally disturb another group's game.
  • Resolve disagreements calmly.
  • Report serious conduct or safety concerns to facility management.
09

Children and Young Players

Children and young players should participate in an appropriate and responsible environment:

  • Registration requirements for coaching must be followed.
  • Parent or guardian information is required for coaching registrations involving players under 18.
  • Parents and guardians should review the applicable registration and participation information.
  • Young players should follow instructions from authorised staff.
  • All participants should treat young players respectfully.
  • Any applicable supervision and consent requirements should be communicated clearly before participation.
Business confirmation pending. A minimum age, supervision ratio, mandatory parent attendance rule or child-protection procedure has not been assumed and must be formally approved by MATCH-BOX.

Football Coaching Session Rules

Confirmed coaching session information:

  • Morning Session: 5:00 AM – 6:30 AM, 90 minutes, planned capacity 10 players (8 outfield players and 2 goalkeepers).
  • Afternoon Session: 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM, 90 minutes, capacity configurable by the administrator.

General coaching guidelines:

  • Players should attend their registered session.
  • Follow the instructions provided during coaching.
  • Respect coaches, staff and other participants.
  • Participate responsibly in drills and activities.
  • Avoid disrupting the session.
  • Follow the applicable registration and cancellation conditions.
Business confirmation pending. Coaching fees, coach qualifications, training equipment and session-specific rules have not been assumed and must be published by the business owner before being presented as binding rules.
