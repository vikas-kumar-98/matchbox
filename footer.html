More than training

MORE THAN TRAINING.
BUILD YOUR GAME.

Match-Box coaching is designed to help players improve the fundamentals of the game in a structured, supportive environment. Sessions focus on technical work, decision-making, and match awareness — the building blocks of confident football.

Whether you're developing your first touch or refining your game for match day, sessions are built around real, pitch-based learning.

  • Technical focus

    Control, passing, and finishing at the heart of every session.

  • Small-sided games

    Realistic scenarios that reinforce learning and match awareness.

  • Goalkeeper training

    Specialist practice for keepers when session capacity allows.

What we work on

EVERY SESSION BUILDS
THE FUNDAMENTALS.

Sessions are designed around the core technical and tactical building blocks of small-sided football.

Ball control & first touch

Close control, receiving under pressure, and confidence in tight spaces.

Passing & receiving

Angles, weight of pass, and combination play with teammates.

Dribbling & ball mastery

Beating a player, changing direction, and moving with the ball.

Shooting & finishing

Striking technique, composure, and decisions in the final third.

Positioning & decisions

Reading the game, making the right choice, and staying ahead of play.

Teamwork & match awareness

Communication, positioning, and understanding the game together.

Fitness & coordination

Movement quality, speed, and physical confidence on the pitch.

Goalkeeper practice

Handling, positioning, footwork, and distribution when available.

Player categories

Categories describe the type of training focus, not age groups or fixed programmes.

Goalkeepers

Developing players

Why train at Match-Box

A STRUCTURED ENVIRONMENT
TO IMPROVE OVER TIME.

Our coaching is designed around the way players actually learn: regular practice, repeated touches, small-sided games, and clear feedback.

  • Practical, pitch-based learning

    No classroom drills — everything happens with the ball at your feet.

  • Repeated ball-contact opportunities

    Every session is designed to get you more touches and more decisions.

  • Small-group format

    Keep every player involved — no standing in line.

  • Game awareness & teamwork

    Understanding the game is built into every drill.
