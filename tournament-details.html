FORMAT & TOURNAMENT RULES

Tournament-specific rules are published by the organiser. Only approved rules are shown here.

4-a-side format clarification: MATCH-BOX uses a 4-a-side football format, meaning four players per team are on the pitch during a match. This does not automatically mean the total squad must contain exactly four players. Squad size, substitute limits, match duration, and other rules come from the specific tournament's approved configuration.
