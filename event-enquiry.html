WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU SUBMIT?

A CLEAR FOUR-STEP PROCESS.

Every event enquiry is reviewed by the MATCH-BOX team. An enquiry is never treated as a confirmed booking.

1

Submit Your Enquiry

Share your event type, preferred date, group size, and requirements.

2

Enquiry Review

The MATCH-BOX team reviews your submitted details.

3

Discuss the Details

The team can follow up to discuss requirements and availability.

4

Confirmation

Your event is confirmed only after approval and confirmation from MATCH-BOX.
